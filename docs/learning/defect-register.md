# Studio defect register

## 2026-11-28 — Playing or switching animations shifted and zoomed the user's camera

Starting playback, seeking, or switching between animation clips (for example `Spin lock wheel` and `Swing vault door` on `dwarven-vault-door`, reported by the owner) mutated the viewport camera by translating `controls.target` and `camera.position` by `clipFocusShift`, resizing `perspectiveCamera.zoom` and `orthoCamera` frustum bounds via `expandedFrame`, clamping `controls.minDistance`/`maxDistance`, and shifting the floor grid and shadow. Any user-selected preset angle (`Front`, `Left`, etc.) or manual orbit/zoom jumped away whenever a clip's swept bounds differed from the rest pose (measured at `27.49` mean RGB diff in `Front` view and `29.02` in `Left` view on `dwarven-vault-door` at identical `t = 0` rest frames). `Viewport` (`src/studio/viewport.ts`) now mounts `this.model` inside an internal `modelStage` wrapper group and never modifies camera position, target, zoom, frustum, orbit limits, or stage floor/shadow when playing, pausing, seeking, or switching clips (and stops interactive part `Test` loops whenever clip playback starts or switches). Low-pose underwater clips whose authored root drops below the rest floor (`Swimming`, `Treading water`, `Slide`) stay cleanly lifted above the floor grid and vertically centered via `clipFloorLift` (`src/studio/framing.ts`) applied only to `modelStage.position.y`, keeping `model.position = [0, 0, 0]`, exported GLB tracks untouched, and non-character models at `0` lift.

`src/studio/framing.test.ts` covers zero `clipFloorLift` across `dwarven-vault-door` clips alongside vertical lifting for submerged humanoid clips across all 87 character animations. `scripts/check-framing.mjs` drives headless Chrome on `dwarven-vault-door` across `Front`, `Left`, custom orbited+zoomed `Perspective`, `Play`/`Pause`, `#playAllClips` auto-advance, and custom orbited+zoomed `Orthographic` views, asserting `< 0.25` mean RGB diff across every clip switch while verifying visible animation from the user's chosen angle alongside the existing `Field Pilot` low-pose and `Field Rover` grounding checks.

## 2026-11-28 — Rotating animated parts and interactive rigs plunged through stationary solids mid-motion

`anvil-hammer-station` (reported by the owner, where `PowerHammer` swung down to `+0.22 rad` and drove the hammer head `18.4cm` straight through the solid steel anvil) and 37 sibling catalog models (`fire-watchtower`, `dome-observatory`, `geothermal-turbine`, `desert-radar-bunker`, `grandfather-clock`, `retro-arcade-cabinet`, `railway-boom-gate`, `rolltop-desk`, `tufted-leather-sofa`, `planetary-stand-mixer`, `mri-gantry-scanner`, `van-de-graaff-generator`, `wooden-cheese-press`, `british-telephone-box`, `toothed-mimic-chest`, `dungeon-hanging-cage`, `grimoire-bookshelf`, `necromancer-bone-throne`, `medbay-diagnostic-scanner`, `hexagonal-shield-emitter`, `orbital-battery-turret`, `neural-link-chair`, `municipal-courthouse`, `timber-grain-silo`, `victorian-conservatory`, `lunar-mining-refinery`, `giant-coastal-sequoia`, `swaying-bamboo-thicket`, `yellow-school-bus`, `vintage-roadster-coupe`, `bank-armored-van`, `mississippi-paddle-steamer`, `alpine-funicular-car`, `delta-wing-starfighter`, `giant-reef-octopus`, `pointed-wizard-hat`, and `farm-tractor`) crashed moving parts straight through their own stationary bodies, reversed hinge swing signs inward into cabinets/fuselages/walls, spun a mounting stanchion through a vehicle roof, overlapped a stern paddlewheel or conveyor into a hull/drum, or dumped a loader bucket underground. The defect slipped past verification because `craft-overlap.ts` only checked solid interpenetration at rest (`t = 0`), `craft-animation.ts` only checked mid-motion detachment/floating rather than mid-motion solid self-collision, and `src/capabilities/sweep.ts` only checks external scene obstacles rather than self-collision against a model's own body. All 38 models were repaired with calibrated strike/hinge pivots, corrected outward hinge signs, exterior boom/paddle/conveyor/visor/tray clearances, single non-duplicated trunks, stationary pulley stanchions, and above-floor dump travel, and verified visually across peak-motion renders in headless Chrome.

`src/models/craft-motion-collision.ts` adds a deterministic whole-class mid-motion solid-collision invariant (`findMidMotionSolidCollisions`) that steps every rotating `AnimationClip` track (across 10–90% timestamps plus every keyframe time) and every non-mimic rotating `riggedPart` slider (`0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%`) using exact closed-mesh volumes and 4×4×4 ray-parity volumetric overlap deltas relative to rest, flagging any opaque solid driving `>= 0.002 m³` and `>= 32%` of its volume newly into another solid body. `src/models/craft-invariants.test.ts` covers both a synthetic blacksmith-anvil-hammer regression (catching a hammer head plunging through an anvil while passing a raised hammer stopping cleanly atop the billet) and zero rotating mid-motion solid collisions across all 612 locked variations inside `npm run verify`.

## 2026-11-27 — Animated slides and hinges detached from their host machines mid-motion

`hydraulic-shop-press` (reported by the owner) and 21 sibling catalog models (`cargo-gantry-crane`, `brass-spyglass`, `rolltop-desk`, `four-poster-bunk-bed`, `twin-basket-fryer`, `benchtop-centrifuge`, `cryogenic-dewar-cart`, `ramen-noodle-stall`, `maple-butcher-block`, `tea-ceremony-table`, `vintage-popcorn-wagon`, `dim-sum-steamer-cart`, `rowing-ergometer`, `tracked-bulldozer`, `viking-dragon-longship`, `sleek-maglev-pod`, `tactical-radio-pack`, `stone-lighthouse`, `farm-tractor`, `castle-gatehouse`, and `coral-jellyfish`) had moving parts that disconnected from their machine mid-animation or along their interactive rig slider (for example, `PressRam`'s `0.34m` rod stroked `-0.34m` straight out of its hydraulic cylinder, floating in mid-air at half-stroke and leaving a `20cm` air gap at full stroke). The defect slipped past verification because `auditModelCraft` only evaluated connectivity at rest (`t = 0`), `inspect:model` and preview posters only captured static rest frames, and AABB-only connectivity missed diagonal/rotated hinge gaps. All 22 models were repaired with full-stroke piston rods, seated telescoping shafts, winch shrouds, drawer runner frames, backsplash lift rails, stationary ceiling trunnions, roof hinge arms, rear ripper frames, hull-seated oars, hinged pot/kettle/steamer lids, and solid tractor/cart axles.

`src/models/craft-animation.ts` adds a deterministic whole-class animation-attachment invariant (`findAnimationDetachments`) that steps every authored `AnimationClip` (across 10–90% timestamps plus every keyframe time) and every interactive `riggedPart` slider (`25–100%`) using OBB-aware `localBoxesTouch` connectivity and per-node rest-anchor tracking, flagging any mid-motion floating component or `> 6cm` break from rest anchor meshes. `src/models/craft-invariants.test.ts` covers both a synthetic hydraulic-press regression (catching a short detaching rod while passing a seated piston rod) and zero detachments across all 612 locked variations inside `npm run verify`.

## 2026-11-27 — Rotating rigged lids and shells reused full overlapping solids occupying the same 3D space

`charcoal-kettle-grill` (reported by the owner) and 7 sibling catalog models (`offset-barrel-smoker`, `visor-astronaut-helmet`, `toothed-mimic-chest`, `treasure-chest`, `grounded-rune-sanctum`, `starport-control-spire`, and `poptop-camper-van`) placed a rotating rigged solid mesh (a second full sphere, full cylinder, solid disk, or roof block) directly inside a stationary body solid at rest. The defect escaped earlier verification because visual judging during the 500-object expansion only sampled 9 assets, static closed-pose thumbnails hid co-located same-color solids behind rims, and `src/models/craft-invariants.ts` had no volumetric interpenetration check (`findExposedZFighting` skips same-material pairs and checks coplanar faces only). All 8 models now use watertight `FrontSide` concave/hollow shells (`src/models/hollow-shells.ts`), concentric curved visors, or hollow encircling collars so both closed and open poses expose clean cavities with zero overlapping solids (and `treasure-chest` also resolved a newly exposed coplanar top-strap Z-fight).

`src/models/craft-overlap.ts` adds a deterministic whole-class volumetric solid-overlap invariant (`findRotatingRigSolidOverlaps`) wired into `auditModelCraft` in `src/models/craft-invariants.ts`. `src/models/craft-invariants.test.ts` asserts both a synthetic regression (catching two co-located spheres under a rotating lid while passing a concave Lathe bowl + hollow Lathe dome lid) and zero rotating-rig solid overlaps across every locked catalog variation in `npm run verify`.

## 2026-11-27 — Combined source and category filters looked like separate tabs with a stale 106 badge

Clicking `Originals 106` while a category filter (such as `Characters`) was active kept the category filter applied with only a faint highlight, showed `Characters` as the heading while the sidebar still displayed `106`, and offered no active-filter chip bar, making the narrowed list look like missing originals. Both sidebar groups now render explicit toggle switches with matched active styling (`SOURCE FILTER` and `CATEGORY FILTER`), source badges display `matching/total` whenever category or search filters narrow them (e.g. `18/106`), `#listingTitle` combines active source and category (`Originals · Characters`), and `#activeFilters` renders removable toggle chips plus a `Clear filters` action above the grid.

`src/studio/filter-state.test.ts` covers `matching / total` badge formatting, combined listing headings, and removable filter chip generation. `scripts/check-inventory.mjs` exercises combined `Originals` + `Characters` filtering in headless Chrome, asserts the `matching / total` badge, combined title, and active filter chips, and verifies clicking the category chip restores all originals.

## 2026-11-26 — PolyFork 429 responses locked out catalog loading for 60 seconds and GLB fetches had no retry or timeout

PolyFork catalog responses do not expose `Retry-After` in `Access-Control-Expose-Headers`, so browsers read `null` and triggered a 60-second client-side blackout after a single HTTP 429, leaving categories like Buildings showing only local originals (such as Signal Tower) and blocking manual Retry clicks. Model GLB downloads also lacked timeouts and transient 429/5xx retries, and an unexpected catalog rejection could leave the shelf stuck on `Loading…`. The catalog client now uses short progressive backoff when `Retry-After` is hidden, deduplicates concurrent page requests, clears backoff on explicit Retry, auto-retries after rate-limit cooldowns, recovers the shelf UI on unexpected failures, and retries/times out transient GLB fetch failures.

`src/data/data.test.ts` and `src/data/browse.test.ts` cover CORS-hidden 429 backoff, manual retry clearing, concurrent request deduplication, and `retryAfterMs` reporting. `src/imports/asset.test.ts` covers transient 429/503 retries for `openPolyforkGlb`. `scripts/check-inventory.mjs` exercises both a CORS-hidden 429 on Buildings (verifying automatic recovery alongside Signal Tower) and a transient 429 when opening a PolyFork GLB.

## 2026-11-26 — Low-pose animations dropped below the viewport and floor grid

Clips whose authored root motion sits below the rest floor (such as Swimming and Treading water) fell partly outside the viewport and behind the floor grid and playback bar because the stage only framed the rest pose. The viewport now samples each clip’s bounding box on selection, shifts the floor, shadow and camera target together while preserving the user’s orbit and zoom, expands the orthographic frame only when a stretched pose exceeds the base frame, and leaves model transforms and exported tracks untouched.

`src/studio/framing.test.ts` covers low-pose offsets, scale invariance and track immutability. `scripts/check-framing.mjs` checks Swimming, Treading water and Slide through the animation picker, confirms user orbit survives clip switches, and verifies exported Hips tracks keep their authored depths.

## 2026-11-26 — Static imported models did not redraw after color edits

Once the on-demand renderer settled on a non-animated GLB, changing a material color, vertex region or tint skipped `stage.invalidate()` unless geometry also changed. Both Properties edits and `window.forgeStudio.setImportEdits` now invalidate the viewport. `scripts/check-remixes.mjs` lets a static model settle and verifies immediate pixel changes from both Properties and the agent API without moving the camera.

## 2026-11-26 — A filtered animation list looked like the entire library
