- 2026-09-17 — iPhone 17 Pro round 11 (loop round five: button seams and measurement integrity). **The button pills read as decals**, and the measured cause was the seam's extent, not its finish: it stood 0.255–0.378 mm outside each pill's silhouette — a 1–2 px near-black ring around the whole pill — and had eaten the rail's flat metal down to 0.042 mm beside it. `SEAM_MARGIN` 0.3 → 0.15 gives a 0.105–0.166 mm band and 0.19 mm of metal back; the worker also *declined* the brief's premise that the pill's finish was wrong, showing the pill face and the rail's flat face render the identical rgb(184,82,50) and that the "glossy rail" is its 0.36 mm chamfer. The seam's tone then went from one fixed near-black to the finish's own albedo at `SEAM_SHADE = 0.2594` (per finish, because a fixed grey fitted on orange rendered 0.89 of Deep Blue's own pill face), taking the seam from 0.09–0.31 of the metal to 0.33–0.56 against a reference crevice at 0.494–0.572, and fixing the lens rings' base hairline which shares the material (0.146 → 0.500 against a reference 0.55–0.60). **The round also earned a measurement-integrity guard**: a worker patched the running app through a repo-root `.env` read by `import.meta.env` (every later capture on that dev server rendered the patched map), and separately found the Vite dev server serving **stale revisions** — four consecutive edits, four byte-identical measurements, a whole ladder invalid with no error. The sweep harness now fingerprints the served bytes of every module twice per sweep, exits 2 on a mismatch, and prints `served <digest>` plus a per-module `revision` line; it reproduced a real stale serve and its own bound is stated in its header. `AGENTS.md` now carries both rules, and `.env`/`.env.*` are gitignored. One item closed as physically expected rather than chased: the left profile's flat dark glass, whose flat mirror sweeps 0.1° of the room across its width, and whose two candidate fixes flattened the right profile and the front too. 54 tests in 9 files, typecheck, lint, build and the look gate all green.
