- 2026-09-17 — iPhone 17 Pro round 8 (loop round two): the look got a gate and an entire feature was found missing. `scripts/check-finishes.mjs` (`npm run check:finishes`) drives its own headless Chrome, reads the `back` view's canvas back for all three finishes and fails when a rail-over-panel luminance ratio leaves ±0.05 of its reference (orange 0.73, blue 0.47, silver 0.87 — measured 0.747/0.508/0.866), with `--graze` scanning a roll close-up for any run of luma ≤ 3 through the page's real pointer input; exit 2 means it could not measure, which is not the same claim as a failed look. `src/iphone/materials.test.ts` pins the look's inputs so CI (no Chrome) still catches drift — two halves of one gate, documented as such in `AGENTS.md`. Fifteen inert `envMapIntensity` values were deleted after confirming nothing reads them (the renderer overwrites them with `scene.environmentIntensity`), with an 11-frame A/B showing 0 of 1,400,000 pixels differ and the served module checked so the A/B could not pass vacuously. The audit found the US variant's **5G mmWave antenna window** missing from the top edge — a 28.76 mm stadium insert, 0.40 of the body width re-derived from the reference photo's own pixels, now built with four gates red-proved five ways, and given its own `mmwave` material (dielectric, roughness 0.82, no clearcoat) that reads 0.833/0.831/0.836 of the frame against the reference's 0.81 and is flat across its face as the real insert is. The audit harness itself was repaired: its 700 ms settle left 93 px of run-to-run variance, so every earlier audit frame — including a round of "no black band" claims — was shot at a slightly non-reproducible pose; it now converges on stillness in frames and three runs are byte-identical, and it refuses a no-op input, a typo'd view, or a page reload mid-sweep. 40 tests in 6 files, typecheck, lint, build, and the look gate all green.

- 2026-09-17 — iPhone 17 Pro round 7 (continuous-improvement loop, first round): the studio environment **had never reached the render** — `scene.environment = null` and the PMREM produced byte-identical frames and `scene.environmentIntensity` moved no pixel, so every round-5/6 material conclusion (including the `metalness: 0.15` fudge's rationale) was measured against black. The PMREM path is replaced by a procedurally drawn equirect canvas, and a second three.js fact came with it: a standard material's own `envMapIntensity` is overwritten by `scene.environmentIntensity` (`WebGLRenderer.js:2694`), so every per-material value in `materials.ts` has never been read. With a working environment the frame went to `metalness: 0.70` and the rail/panel luminance ratios landed inside ±0.05 for the first time (orange 0.747 vs 0.73, blue 0.508 vs 0.47 — the round-6 0.09 miss closed — silver 0.866 vs 0.87), the graze return of the black band stayed at 0 px of luma ≤ 3, and the back panel gained a real gradient. Three looks the new environment then broke were fixed in a corrective slice, each after naming its mechanism: the front was a uniform grey slab because a flat mirror 1702 mm from a wall-sized source sweeps only 2.4° and returns one value (now two small cards inside that cone — mean 64, p02 44, max 110); the ring glints were Fresnel at grazing incidence, not the environment (ring roughness 0.42, 0 px ≥ 235, was 41/63/59 px); and silver's frame at roughness 0.92 fell out of band (0.670) and is back at 0.42/0.866 with a per-finish panel lift. MagSafe was proved to draw no pixel at all by painting it pure red and diffing — it is invisible because it is buried, not because of its roughness step. New tool `scripts/review-lanes.mjs` probes the codex/claude reviewer lanes with evidence (both currently blocked: codex's pinned model needs a newer CLI, claude's OAuth session expired) and `AGENTS.md` now requires that probe before any claim that independent review is unavailable. 32 tests in 5 files, typecheck, lint, build green; poster re-shot.
