# Form Foundry and the PolyFork website

Checked on 26 November 2026 in a signed-out desktop browser. The comparison covers the visible website controls, the published download index, the studio controls, and representative downloaded assets. Paid creator edits and paid download contents were not exercised.

| Workflow | PolyFork website | Form Foundry |
|---|---|---|
| Find a humanoid | The Characters gallery lists 353 rigged humanoids. | Inventory contains the full roster, two originals and one additional catalog character with no retargetable skeleton. The previous filter misplaced 146 humanoids under Animals. [Characters](https://polyfork.dev/characters) |
| Animate a character | The live creator offers 94 named motions and A/T rest poses. The public CC0 download index contains 85 of those motions. | Compatible imported rigs get the 85 downloadable motions. The two originals retain their own Idle and Walk as well. The inspector can search, preview and scrub clips. GLB export includes the available motions. [Creator](https://polyfork.dev/create) · [Download index](https://polyfork.dev/cdn/anim-index.json) |
| Customize a body or outfit | The creator exposes garment slots, individual color zones, body shape, age and expression. Its signed-out page allows viewing and animation and identifies editing as a subscription feature. Animals have a separate creator. | Remixes support painting individual materials and authored vertex-color regions, overall tint, scale and height. The two original suits also expose two colors, build proportions, details and mesh quality. Garment swaps and facial expression controls remain gaps. [Character creator](https://polyfork.dev/create) · [Animal creator](https://polyfork.dev/animal-creator.php) |
| Customize or pose an object | The Survey Drone page exposes arm span, rotor size, leg stance, hull width, per-part colors and articulated rotor/gimbal controls. The mesh has five named pivots and zero embedded animation clips. | Imported objects support material and vertex-region paint, whole-model sizing and playback of embedded clips. A saved remix keeps its own edits. Author-defined mechanical pivots and IK still lack editing controls. [Survey Drone](https://polyfork.dev/asset/survey-drone-7c71f9) |
| Assemble a game environment | Kits group matching assets. The scene editor can duplicate and transform objects, sculpt or paint terrain, change lighting and export a scene. | Inventory and Object Studio work on one asset at a time. Kit browsing, multi-object placement, terrain and scene export are missing. [Kits](https://polyfork.dev/kits) · [Scene editor](https://polyfork.dev/editor) |
| Deliver and share assets | Asset pages offer procedural modules, engine integration examples, share/embed links and additional formats. The pricing page advertises HD/PBR editions. | GLB export works for models and motion. The six originals also have editable JSON recipes, a three.js SDK and an HD geometry setting. Imported catalog models use the free meshes. The studio has no equivalent parametric module export for those imports, catalog-wide HD, FBX/USD export or shareable edited-scene links. [Asset page](https://polyfork.dev/asset/taverna-waiter-base) · [Pricing](https://polyfork.dev/pricing) |

The nine additional motions seen in the public preview picker were Drunk idle, Drinking, Laughing, Punch, Kick, Thriller, Swing, Twist and Hip hop. They did not appear in the publisher's downloadable CC0 index at the time of verification. [Character creator](https://polyfork.dev/create) · [Download index](https://polyfork.dev/cdn/anim-index.json)
